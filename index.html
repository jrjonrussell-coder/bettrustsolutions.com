Practice

Thirty years at the operational centre of regulated sports futures markets and regulated betting

StraightForecast is an independent practice founded by Jonathan Russell. The career began in Securities and Futures Authority regulated spread betting at William Hill Index and Ladbrokes Sporting Spreads, trading sporting outcomes as contracts for differences. That is the closest British antecedent of the event contract markets now taking shape in the United States, and it is why the questions being asked there are familiar rather than novel.

From there to South Africa to establish the trading department of the first corporate sports betting operation on the continent, then Victor Chandler Index in Gibraltar with the team assembled by Tony Bloom before Starlizard, then Spreadex. At Sportingbet, part of the team that built the first automated in-play and derivative markets in the regulated sector.

At Betway, Global Head of Trading for a decade, building the function across more than fifty sports and forty licensed territories, followed by board appointments across the group's regulated entities in Europe, Latin America, Canada and Africa.

Three consecutive terms as elected Non-Executive Chair of the International Betting Integrity Association, leading its transition from European to global scope and working directly with the International Olympic Committee, UEFA and the International Tennis Integrity Agency on coordination protocols. That was followed by a Senior Advisor role at IC360, spanning integrity analysis, regulatory positioning and monitoring architecture.

Filings

Regulatory submissions

Comments filed in the United States Commodity Futures Trading Commission rulemaking on prediction markets. All are on the public docket at comments.cftc.gov.

18 July 2026  ·  RIN 3038-AF65  ·  91 Fed. Reg. 35,806

Prediction Markets: Public Interest Determinations

Comment on the proposed public interest determination factors, addressing manipulation susceptibility, the misuse of material non-public information, and the absence of any reciprocal obligation on the bodies that control the underlying events. Approximately 2,850 words across nine sections.

Companion comment on sporting event contracts

Comment addressing the specific integrity questions arising where event contracts settle on sporting outcomes, and the interface between venue-held trading data and the disciplinary processes of sports governing bodies.
